Living as Practical Atheists
When Faith Doesn't Touch Daily Life
Opening Question
How often do we claim faith in God
yet live as if He doesn't exist?
The Disconnect
We believe in theory. We doubt in practice.
This is practical atheism.
Today's Journey
Let's explore how we move from practical atheism
to practicing faith in everyday life.
Scripture Foundation
Hebrews 11:13-16
Our spiritual ancestors lived differently
Hebrews 11:13
"All these people were still living by faith when they died. They did not receive the things promised; they only saw them and welcomed them from a distance, admitting that they were foreigners and strangers on earth."
Hebrews 11:14-16
"People who say such things show that they are looking for a country of their own... Instead, they were longing for a better country—a heavenly one."
The Key Truth
God prepared a city for those who live
as foreigners here, citizens there.
What Is Practical Atheism?
It's believing God exists intellectually
but living daily as if He doesn't.
The Sunday-Monday Gap
Sunday morning we sing "God is sovereign!"
Monday morning we're paralyzed by anxiety. 😰
The Prayer Paradox
We have direct access to the Creator of the universe,
yet we exhaust every human solution first.
Story: The Mother's Breakdown
Rachel prays beautifully at church.
At home, she lies awake terrified her teenage son
is hanging with the wrong crowd.
Rachel's Realization
"I was trying to secure his future myself
instead of trusting the God who saved me.
My fear showed I believed more in the world's power
to destroy than God's power to save."
Application:
Where are you trying to control
instead of trust?
The Core Problem
We forget heaven is real.
We forget this world is temporary.
We live like this is all there is.
C.S. Lewis on Our Blindness
"If you read history you will find that the Christians who did most for the present world were just those who thought most of the next."
*C.S. Lewis — Challenges our earthly focus — Shows eternity-minded living changes today*
James 2:19
"You believe that there is one God. Good! Even the demons believe that—and shudder."
Knowledge vs. Trust
Bart Ehrman is a renowned Bible scholar who teaches
at major universities and has written bestselling books
about Scripture.
Head Knowledge vs. Heart Trust
Despite being a world-renowned Bible scholar,
Ehrman is an atheist. His life demonstrates that
head knowledge is not the same as saving faith.
Demons and Scholars
Demons have perfect theology. Ehrman has vast biblical knowledge.
Both lack the one thing that matters: trust.

Part 1: Symptoms of Practical Atheism
Let's get specific about how we live
without God in our daily decisions
Symptom #1: Financial Panic
We say "God will provide" at church.
We check our bank balance fifty times on Tuesday.
The 3 AM Wake-Up
You know the feeling—bolt awake at 3 AM
thinking about bills, medical expenses, retirement.
Where's God in that moment?
Matthew 6:25-26
"Therefore I tell you, do not worry about your life... Look at the birds of the air; they do not sow or reap or store away in barns, and yet your heavenly Father feeds them. Are you not much more valuable than they?"
The Worry Test
Here's the uncomfortable truth: our anxiety level
reveals what we really believe about God.
Symptom #2: Horizontal Vision
When problems arise, we focus all our attention on our problems
instead of looking up to our Problem-Solver.
Remember Peter on the Water?
Peter actually walks on water!
As long as his eyes stay on Jesus,
the impossible becomes possible. 🌊
Matthew 14:30
"But when he saw the wind, he was afraid and, beginning to sink, cried out, 'Lord, save me!' You of little faith, why did you doubt?"
Our Daily Waters
Every problem is our chance to choose:
Will we look at the waves or the Wave-Walker?
Symptom #3: Self-Reliance
We treat God like our emergency backup plan
instead of our first response.
Story: The Marriage Crisis
Tom and Linda's marriage was falling apart.
They tried counseling, self-help books, trial separation.
Prayer? That came last.
Tom's Admission
"We spent $5,000 on therapy before we spent
five minutes together on our knees."
Proverbs 3:5-6
"Trust in the LORD with all your heart and lean not on your own understanding; in all your ways submit to him, and he will make your paths straight."
The Control Illusion
We act like we're the captain of our ship,
and God is just the emergency rescue service.
Application
What problem are you handling alone
that you should bring to God first?
The Approval Addiction
We sacrifice conviction for comfort,
truth for acceptance, integrity for inclusion.
Symptom #4: Security in Stuff
Our peace rises and falls with our bank balance.
Our identity comes from our achievements.
The Modern Idols
  • Financial security
  • Children's Achievements
  • Comfort
  • Social Media image
The Heart Check
Want to know what you really worship?
Look at your bank statement and calendar.

Part 2: Citizens vs. Sojourners
Understanding who we really are
changes how we really live
What's a Sojourner?
A sojourner is someone living temporarily in a place
that's not their permanent home—fully present but
never fully settled.
1 Peter 2:11-12
"Dear friends, I urge you, as foreigners and exiles, to abstain from sinful desires which wage war against your soul. Live such good lives among the pagans that they may see your good deeds and glorify God."
Resident Aliens
Peter calls us "foreigners and exiles"—
we belong here physically, but our true citizenship is elsewhere.
We Are In the World—But Not Of It
This is our identity.
This reorders our priorities.
Philippians 3:20
"But our citizenship is in heaven. And we eagerly await a Savior from there..."
Think About It This Way
You're visiting another country when suddenly
war breaks out with your homeland.
Whose side are you on?
No Question, Right?
You'd never fight against your own country,
even if you're currently standing on enemy soil.
The Spiritual War
We're in enemy territory every day.
Every sin is like taking up arms
against our true homeland.
Ephesians 6:12
"For our struggle is not against flesh and blood, but against the rulers, against the authorities, against the powers of this dark world and against the spiritual forces of evil in the heavenly realms."
Choosing Sides Daily
When we gossip, we side with the enemy.
When we forgive, we fight for the Kingdom.
Think About Foreigners
When you see visitors from another country in your town,
you can tell they're different—how they dress, how they speak.
You just know they're not from here.
We Should Look Different Too
If we're truly citizens of heaven, people should be able
to tell. Our priorities, speech, reactions—all different.
Story: Ava the Refugee
Ava fled persecution in Iran five years ago.
She became a Christian there—a crime punishable by death.
She lost everything to follow Jesus.
Standing Out in America
Now in our city, Ava still stands out.
Not just her accent. It's something deeper—
she doesn't grumble, she finds gratitude, she forgives immediately.
The Questions Start
"Why aren't you bitter after what happened?"
"How can you be happy after everything you've been through?"
"Do you ever regret it?"
The Cost She Paid
Ava's family disowned her. The Iranian government
seized her parents' home. Her brother won't speak to her.
She can never go back.
Her Response
"I lost my earthly family to gain God as my Father.
I lost my country to gain heaven as my home.
I lost my old life to gain eternal life."
She Lives Like a Sojourner
Ava works hard. Pays bills. Grocery shops.
But everyone knows—she's just passing through.
Her treasure is somewhere else. 💎
Then There's Daniel
Exiled in Babylon. Prominent government official.
Prayed three times daily with his window open—
everyone could see him worshiping the true God.
Daniel 6:10
"Now when Daniel learned that the decree had been published, he went home to his upstairs room where the windows opened toward Jerusalem. Three times a day he got down on his knees and prayed, giving thanks to his God, just as he had done before."
He Knew the Cost
The decree meant death for anyone who prayed.
Daniel prayed anyway—same time, same place, windows open.
Not hiding. Not compromising.
The Lion's Den
They threw him to the lions for his devotion to God.
But God shut the lions' mouths.
Daniel's faith was vindicated before the whole empire.
Blood Then, Backlash Now
Ava suffered for Christ. Daniel faced lions.
Early Christians were imprisoned and executed.
Today: canceling, adoption denials, broken friendships, lawsuits, vandalism.
Where Are Our Lions?
Why Aren't You or I Being Persecuted?
Maybe it's because people can't tell the difference
between us and the world. We blend in too well.
The Blending Problem
Same entertainment. Same language. Same priorities.
Same anxiety. Same anger. Same gossip.
How would anyone know we're different? 🤔
John 15:18-19
"If the world hates you, keep in mind that it hated me first. If you belonged to the world, it would love you as its own. As it is, you do not belong to the world... That is why the world hates you."
Unmolested
If your faith never makes anyone uncomfortable,
if your life never challenges anyone's sin,
if your devotion is completely invisible—
maybe you've settled too comfortably.
DISCUSSION QUESTION
When was the last time your faith

Two Ways to Live
Citizens cling to this world.
Sojourners prepare for the next.
The Citizen's Investment
Accumulate wealth. Chase comfort. Seek status.
Make this world your treasure.
The Sojourner's Investment
Store up heaven's riches. Make eternal impact.
Invest in souls, not stuff.
Actions of a Sojourner
Our deeds should reflect our "true home."
Being resident aliens means engaging the world—
but not conforming morally to it.
1 Peter 2:12
Live such good lives among the pagans that, though they accuse you of doing wrong, they may see your good deeds and glorify God on the day he visits us.
Living Honorably Among Unbelievers
Excellence at work—not for promotion, but for witness.
Integrity in business—even when it costs.
Kindness to enemies—because we know eternity.
Story: The Honest Contractor
Mike's company could've hidden the mistake.
The client would never know. An extra $8,000 in profit—
just stay quiet.
Mike's Decision
He called the client. Explained the billing error.
Refunded the money. His crew thought he was crazy.
"Why throw away $8,000?"
The Eternal Perspective
"Because I'm not building my kingdom here.
I'm representing Another Kingdom.
Eight thousand dollars won't matter in heaven,
but my integrity will.
I'm not carrying the devil's money into eternity."
The Ripple Effect
The client was stunned. Two months later, that client
showed up at Mike's church. "I want to know the God
who makes people that honest."
Application
Sojourners act differently—not for temporal gain
but because we already have eternal gain.
Abraham's Revolutionary Choice
Left comfort in Ur for tents in Canaan.
Why would anyone do that?
Hebrews 11:10
"For he was looking forward to the city with foundations, whose architect and builder is God."
The Tent Mentality
Abraham never built a house. Always lived in tents.
Ready to move when God said "Go."
Jonathan Edwards on Pilgrims
"God has redeemed you out of the world, and therefore do not live as though you had your portion in this life. Live as pilgrims and strangers."
*Jonathan Edwards — Calls out worldly attachment — Challenges us to live like Abraham, not settlers*
What Are Your Tents?
What are you holding loosely?
What could you leave behind tomorrow?
Charles Spurgeon on Hurrying Through
"We are hurrying through this world... we are in this country, not as residents, but only as visitors."
*Charles Spurgeon — Emphasizes temporary status — Warns against settling into this world*
2 Corinthians 4:17-18
"For our light and momentary troubles are achieving for us an eternal glory... So we fix our eyes not on what is seen, but on what is unseen, since what is seen is temporary, but what is unseen is eternal."
The Eternal Math
Present pain: Light and momentary
Future glory: Weighty and eternal
Do the math!
Guarding Against Settling
The temptation is real: make this world our home.
Get comfortable. Build our kingdom here.
Forget we're just passing through.
Signs We're Settling
We panic over temporary losses.
We sacrifice eternity for comfort now.
We forget to ask, "How does this matter in 10,000 years?"

Part 3: Seeing the Unseen Reality
Learning to trust what we cannot see
more than what we can
Hebrews 11:1
"Now faith is confidence in what we hope for and assurance about what we do not see."
The Two Realities
There's what our eyes see—bills, sickness, conflict.
There's what faith sees—provision, purpose, promises.
The Elisha Story
The king of Aram sends an army to capture one prophet—Elisha.
They surround the city at night. Early morning, Elisha's servant
sees the enemy. "Oh no, my lord! What shall we do?" 😱
Elisha's Calm Response
"Don't be afraid. Those who are with us
are more than those who are with them."
Elisha's Prayer
"Open his eyes, Lord, so that he may see."
Not "Send help" but "Show the help already here."
The Spiritual Reality
2 Kings 6:17—"Then the LORD opened the servant's eyes,
and he looked and saw the hills full of horses and
chariots of fire all around Elisha."
Our Spiritual Blindness
We see cancer, not God's comfort.
We see bankruptcy, not God's provision.
We see betrayal, not God's justice coming.
What If We Could See?
Angels dispatched at every prayer.
God's hand restraining greater disasters.
Heaven's armies fighting our battles. 👀
Colossians 3:1-2
"Since, then, you have been raised with Christ, set your hearts on things above... Set your minds on things above, not on earthly things."
The Address Change
Your body lives on earth.
But where does your mind live?
Your heart? Your hope?
Story: The Diagnosis
"The cancer is aggressive," the doctor said.
Janet smiled. "So is my God."
Janet's Perspective
"Cancer can take my body—it's temporary anyway.
It can't touch my soul—that belongs to Jesus."
Through chemo, she ministered to nurses.
Through dying, she showed others how to live.
Romans 8:18
"I consider that our present sufferings are not worth comparing with the glory that will be revealed in us."
Application
Compare your pain to coming glory, not to others' lives.
It's no contest!

Part 4: From Theory to Practice
Practical steps to stop living
as practical atheists
Six Practical Steps
1. Honest Confession
2. Daily Remembrance
3. Practice His Presence
4. Vertical Before Horizontal
5. Bank on Promises
6. Eternity Perspective
First: Honest Confession
"Lord, I've been living like You're not real.
Like heaven's not real. Like this world is everything."
A Father's Desperation
A man brings his demon-possessed son to Jesus.
He asked Jesus to help his son.
Jesus tells him, "Everything is possible for one who believes."
The Most Honest Prayer in Scripture
Mark 9:24
Immediately the boy's father exclaimed, "I do believe; help me overcome my unbelief!"
Second: Daily Remembrance
Every morning, before your feet hit the floor:
"I'm a citizen of heaven on assignment today."
Make It Visible
Write "Temporary" on your calendar.
Put "Eternal" on your Bible.
Which gets more attention?
How Sojourners Handle Daily Stress
When you see yourself as a sojourner, stress changes
from threat to opportunity. Your worry list gets rewritten.
Citizens Worry About
Status. Stuff. Image. Comfort.
What people think.
Basically, Protecting the kingdom they're building here.
Sojourners Worry About
Faithfulness. Holiness. Others' souls.
Am I representing Christ well? Am I investing in what lasts?
Matthew 6:19-21
"Do not store up for yourselves treasures on earth, where moths and vermin destroy... But store up for yourselves treasures in heaven. For where your treasure is, there your heart will be also."
Third: Practice His Presence
God isn't watching from heaven.
He's here. In this room. In your car.
In that difficult conversation.
Psalm 139:7-8
"Where can I go from your Spirit? Where can I flee from your presence? If I go up to the heavens, you are there; if I make my bed in the depths, you are there."
Fourth: Vertical Before Horizontal
When problems hit, train yourself:
Look up before you look around. ⬆️
The New Reflex
Old pattern: Problem → Panic → Maybe pray
New pattern: Problem → Prayer → Peace
Fifth: Bank on Promises
Stop treating God's promises like nice poetry.
Start treating them like guaranteed checks.
Story: The Single Mom's Faith
Katie's income: $2,000 monthly. Bills: $2,500.
She quoted Philippians 4:19 daily. Every month, somehow,
everything got paid. "God's math doesn't follow earth's rules."
Isaiah 41:10
"So do not fear, for I am with you; do not be dismayed, for I am your God. I will strengthen you and help you; I will uphold you with my righteous right hand."
Not Wishful Thinking
This is the Creator of the universe
making you a personal promise.
Will He keep His word?
Sixth: Eternity Perspective
Before every decision ask:
"How will this matter in 10,000 years?"
The Forever Filter
That hobby that owns you? That grudge you won't drop?
Run it through the forever filter.
Most things shrink to actual size.
The Rope Illustration
Watch this powerful picture of eternity versus
our brief time on earth:
1 John 2:17
"The world and its desires pass away, but whoever does the will of God lives forever."
The Community Factor
Practical atheism thrives in isolation.
Faith grows in community.
Hebrews 10:24-25
"Let us consider how we may spur one another on toward love and good deeds, not giving up meeting together... but encouraging one another."
The Daily Decision
Every morning we choose:
Live as if God is distant?
Or live as if God is here?
Remember Who You Are
You're not a permanent resident here.
You're an ambassador from another Kingdom.
World Changing Truth
This world is not your home.
Heaven is real. God is here.
Live like you believe it.
The Choice Before Us
Keep living as practical atheists—stressed, fearful, grasping?
Or start living as practicing believers—peaceful in problems,
confident in crisis, secure in the Savior.
Your Next Step
Tomorrow morning, before you do anything else:
"Lord, I'm a citizen of heaven. Help me live like it."
Final Thought
You're not home yet.
The best is still to come.
Start living like you believe it. 🏠
