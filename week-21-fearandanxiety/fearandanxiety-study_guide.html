A Powerful Prophecy about Fear and Anxiety

Voddie Baucham

WATCH VIDEO

Summary

In this message from Exodus 2:23–25, Voddie Baucham addresses the fear and anxiety that mark our times—from pandemics and wars to personal losses and unanswered prayers. Rather than offering quick fixes or pretending everything is fine, he walks through four words from the text that anchor us in the midst of ongoing suffering: God heard, God remembered, God saw, and God knew.

These four realities reveal what is happening in the "vertical" even when we can only see the "horizontal"—our pain, our groaning, and our unanswered questions. The sermon culminates at the cross, where God demonstrated once and for all that He hears, remembers, sees, and knows His people, and is so committed to our redemption that He crushed and killed His only Son to guarantee it.

In the midst of our deepest fears and anxieties, we can find comfort knowing that God hears our groaning, remembers His covenant, sees our suffering, and knows exactly what we need—even when we can't see His work in our lives.

Main Points

  1. We shouldn't live like fake Christians who pretend everything is always fine when we're actually suffering.
  2. Fear and anxiety are real struggles that won't just disappear tomorrow—we need to learn how to deal with them while they're still present.
  3. God hears our groaning even when it feels like He's not listening to our prayers.
  4. God remembers His covenant with us, not because we deserve it, but because of His faithfulness.
  5. God sees everything that happens to us—He's never absent or distracted during our worst moments.
  6. God knows intimately what we're going through and what we truly need, even better than we do.
  7. God's sovereignty means He's either in control of everything or nothing—we can't trust Him for salvation if we can't trust Him in suffering.
  8. The cross proves God's commitment to us—if He gave His Son for our redemption, He'll bring us through whatever we're facing.
