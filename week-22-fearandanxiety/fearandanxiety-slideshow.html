Key Truth
In the midst of our deepest fears and anxieties, we can find comfort knowing that God hears our groaning, remembers His covenant, sees our suffering, and knows exactly what we need—even when we can't see His work in our lives.
1
We shouldn't live like fake Christians who pretend everything is always fine when we're actually suffering.
2
Fear and anxiety are real struggles that won't just disappear tomorrow—we need to learn how to deal with them while they're still present.
3
God hears our groaning even when it feels like He's not listening to our prayers.
4
God remembers His covenant with us, not because we deserve it, but because of His faithfulness.
5
God sees everything that happens to us—He's never absent or distracted during our worst moments.
6
God knows intimately what we're going through and what we truly need, even better than we do.
7
God's sovereignty means He's either in control of everything or nothing—we can't trust Him for salvation if we can't trust Him in suffering.
8
The cross proves God's commitment to us—if He gave His Son for our redemption, He'll bring us through whatever we're facing.
